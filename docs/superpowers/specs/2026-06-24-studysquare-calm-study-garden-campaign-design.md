# StudySquare Calm Study Garden Campaign Design

## Goal

Create a mixed marketing campaign pack for StudySquare that explains what learners and teachers get from the product while giving the brand a calm, memorable visual world. The campaign should combine AI-generated nature imagery with StudySquare product language and interface-inspired design, using blue skies, green grass, and serene outdoor learning scenes as the campaign backdrop.

## Product Truths To Advertise

StudySquare is positioned as a playful academic studio for students, exam-prep learners, and teachers. The campaign should advertise these benefits:

- AI Assessment Builder: choose subject, topic, difficulty, and question count; generate a curriculum-aligned quiz quickly.
- StudyLab: a spatial AI tutor where topics become connected cards, steps, examples, and explanations.
- Learning Gap Map: weak areas are mapped visually so learners know what to fix next.
- Interactive Study Mode: match, order, categorize, and fill-the-gap activities make revision more active.
- Instant AI Feedback: answers are graded quickly with clear explanations.
- Curriculum and Exam Alignment: supports WAEC, JAMB, NECO, SAT, ACT, IGCSE, Cambridge, national syllabi, and textbook-aligned practice.
- Progress Analytics: learners and teachers can see subject performance and improvement over time.
- Teacher Tools: teachers can generate assessments, assign them to classes, grade submissions, and review class progress.
- Pricing Hook: two free assessments to start, then a simple monthly offer.

## Campaign Concept

Name: Calm Study Garden

Core idea: StudySquare turns stressful study into a clear path through a bright learning garden. The sky represents clarity, the grass represents progress, and StudySquare's square tiles become learning blocks, stepping stones, floating study cards, trail markers, and product panels.

The campaign should feel optimistic, peaceful, and academic without becoming childish. It should look like students are walking into exam confidence, not being buried under content.

## Visual Direction

Use AI-generated raster backgrounds as the visual base:

- bright blue skies with soft daylight
- green grass, gentle hills, trees, flowers, and open outdoor study spaces
- natural paths, stepping stones, or garden clearings
- clean negative space for headlines
- no gloomy lighting, cluttered classrooms, harsh exam stress, or generic office SaaS visuals

Layer StudySquare brand elements on top:

- cyan/blue StudySquare square mark and wordmark
- floating white product cards with soft shadows
- modular four-tile square motifs
- lime and mint marker highlights
- small exam badges and subject chips
- product screenshots or screenshot-inspired panels from existing app references where useful

Primary palette:

- brand ink: `#111318`
- brand sky: `#A9D2FF`
- brand blue: `#4D7CFF`
- Brain Ink cyan: `#13A7DF`
- mint: `#BDF7B2`
- lime: `#D7FF70`
- orange accent: `#FF9A66`
- paper white: `#F4F4F0`

Typography:

- Display tone should follow StudySquare's chunky, friendly headline style.
- Body copy should stay short, readable, and direct.
- Ad text should avoid long paragraphs.

## Copy Rules

- Keep headlines short and benefit-led.
- Speak to students, parents, and teachers without sounding corporate.
- Use "StudySquare" as the platform brand and "StudyLab" as a product feature.
- Avoid vague AI hype. Tie AI to concrete results: quiz creation, feedback, gap mapping, explanations, and teacher time saved.
- CTA options: Start learning free, Build your first quiz, Open StudyLab, Fix your next gap.

## Image Generation Prompt Pattern

Use case: ads-marketing

Asset type: StudySquare campaign background

Primary request: serene educational nature scene for StudySquare marketing, bright blue sky, green grass, calm outdoor learning environment

Scene/backdrop: open garden, study path, outdoor classroom, or learning field with clear negative space

Subject: study cards, notebooks, tablets, learning tiles, or exam path elements integrated naturally

Style/medium: polished editorial advertising image, semi-realistic with warm educational art direction

Composition/framing: leave clean space for headline and StudySquare product panels

Lighting/mood: sunny, optimistic, calm, fresh, confident

Color palette: blue sky, green grass, white paper, cyan brand accents, mint and lime highlights

Text: no generated text inside the image unless explicitly needed; add final text in layout for accuracy

Constraints: no watermarks, no distorted logos, no unreadable generated typography, no dark or stressful exam-room atmosphere

## Implementation Approach

1. Generate or compose reusable nature background plates for the campaign.
2. Build poster/ad layouts with deterministic HTML/CSS or image composition so brand text stays crisp.
3. Use existing StudySquare logo components, colors, and product screenshots as reference material.
4. Export each final format as PNG for sharing and print-ready PDF/PNG for poster formats.
5. Visually verify at least the main social and poster outputs for text fit, image clarity, and brand consistency.

## Success Criteria

- Assets clearly look like StudySquare, not generic education stock.
- Each feature poster communicates one concrete benefit within a few seconds.
- The nature direction is visible: blue sky, green grass, calm outdoor learning.
- Text is crisp and not embedded through image generation when accuracy matters.
- Social and print formats are share-ready.
- The campaign can be extended later without reinventing the visual system.
